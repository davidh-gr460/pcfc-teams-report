Team Requirements & Compliance

Team Register

  • Lens Definitions: Girls = female youth teams (U8–U18). Boys = male youth teams (U12–U19). Mixed = mixed youth teams. Adult Women = Ladies and Cougars sides. Adult Men = Senior First and Vets. Disability = any Pan Disability team (incl. the U18 side), which is shown as its own lens rather than by gender.
  • Age Bands: U19 teams are counted as youth. The Pan Disability teams L2, L3, L4 and I11's have no age in their name and are treated as adult/open age.
  • Player Figures: "Players" is the first figure in each team's record; "Registered" and "Allocated" are the labelled figures. Headline totals use Registered. Where Players is lower than Registered the gap is shown in the register.
  • County Cups: only the single county cup listed in the extract is counted. U12 Maroons, Raptors and Sharks (Sunday) had further competitions hidden behind "Show more".
  • Penalty points were not visible in the extract so are not reported.
